# Code of conduct

We want this project to be a respectful place for everyone who takes part.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not on the person.
- Use clear and simple words. Many of us do not speak English as a first language.
- Respect the time of others. Keep issues and pull requests focused.

## What we do not accept

- Insults, threats, harassment or discrimination of any kind.
- Personal attacks, and public or private publication of the private information of others.
- Any other conduct that a reasonable person would call unprofessional.

## Enforcement

The maintainers can edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules.
They can ban a person from the project for repeated or serious breaks.
To report a problem, use the private reporting form in the **Security** tab and say that it is a conduct report.
The maintainers keep the identity of the reporter private.

This code is based on the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1 (https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
